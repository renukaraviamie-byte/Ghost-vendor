CASE SOLVED

The truth about Brightway Traders

Auditor Meera Rao spotted invoice INV-1046 booked three times, then found that vendor Sunrise Supplies was an abandoned shed. The bank trail showed six "vendor" payments, ₹7,24,450 in total, all landing in the Accounts Manager's own account. Shown the evidence file, he confessed.

Lesson: Fraud hides in neat-looking books. Duplicate entries, vendors with no real premises, and payments funnelled into one staff account are classic red flags.
